Our mission

Growth Empowerment Success equips students, families, and educators with the academic, social-emotional, and mental wellness tools to turn potential into sustained achievement and wellbeing.

Empowerment is a skill. It can be taught, practiced, and measured. That is why our work is built on steady coaching relationships, practical skill-building, and honest accountability, not one-time motivational events.

Who we serve

Students and families

Students from upper elementary through college, and the parents who support them, looking for academic and social-emotional coaching.

Schools and districts

Counseling and student-support offices that want workshops for students or practical SEL and wellness training for staff.

Nonprofits and agencies

Youth-serving programs and community or government initiatives that need contracted programming with outcomes they can report.

Services

One toolkit, delivered in person or virtually, one-on-one or in groups, to fit your budget and setting.

Individual, group, and family coaching

One-on-one, small-group, and family sessions on academic skills, self-regulation, goal-setting, and confidence, in person or virtual.

School professional development and organization workshops

Practical SEL and wellness training for teachers, counselors, and youth workers, plus workshops for students at schools, after-school programs, and community organizations.

Consulting and contracting

Custom program design and delivery for schools, districts, nonprofits, and agencies, including outcome tracking and reporting.

Featured: educator wellness workshop

A flexible one-day professional development experience for teachers and school staff on burnout prevention, stress management, and social-emotional wellness. It can be paired with lighter follow-up sessions through the year to keep skills growing.

Participants leave able to:

  • Recognize early signs of burnout in themselves and colleagues
  • Manage stress in the moment and across a school year
  • Calm and regulate a classroom or workspace under pressure
  • Use SEL principles in daily interactions with students and each other
  • Build lasting wellness habits and know where to turn for support

Includes breakout sessions by role or grade level.

How we work together

Whether we are supporting one student or a whole district, you stay in the driver's seat and we measure the growth together.

  1. Let's talk

    Share your story and your goals. Together we decide what support fits you best.

  2. Make a plan

    We put it in writing: what we'll work on, when, and the cost, so everyone starts on the same page.

  3. Build skills

    Coaching, workshops, or training that grow the strengths you already have.

  4. See your progress

    We check in on real results together, from confidence and engagement to goals reached.

  5. Celebrate and grow

    We reflect on what's working, celebrate your wins, and choose your next steps.
